Maya's Coding Adventure!

A Brownie Scout learns about coding!

🌼

Meet Maya! 👋

Maya is a Brownie Scout who loves adventures!

Today, Maya has a big mission: get ready for her Girl Scout meeting! But first, she needs a plan.

Did you know computers need plans, too?

They're called algorithms (al-go-ri-th-ims)!

Algorithms are lists of instructions that tell computers what to do.

First, Next, Last 🎒

Maya needs to:

1. Wake up! ⏰

2. Put on her Brownie vest! 👕

3. Go to her meeting! 🌟

Maya has to do things in the right order.

In coding, this is called sequencing! Computers follow instructions in order, just like Maya!

Again and Again 🔁

Every week, Maya gets ready for her Girl Scout meeting.

Wake up. Get dressed. Go to her meeting!

Instead of writing the same instructions again and again, a programmer can tell a computer to repeat them.

That's called a loop!

Oops! A Bug! 🐛

Oh no! Maya's plan says to go to her meeting before getting dressed!

That doesn't make sense!

Maya spots the mistake and fixes her plan.

Programmers call mistakes in code bugs (bugs are little mistakes)! Finding and fixing them is called debugging!

You're a Coder! 💻

Maya learned three coding superpowers!

🌟 Sequencing: Put instructions in order.

🔁 Loops: Repeat instructions.

🐛 Debugging: Find and fix mistakes.

When you tell a computer what to do, you're coding!

Now it's your turn! Help Maya get ready by playing the sequencing game! 🎮
